# ADR 0002: Minimal unverified catalog and neutral assets

Status: accepted

The brief supplies brand and can-line names but not a complete cited catalog. CANVAULT seeds only those names, marks them unverified and keeps colors as a separate empty import. No barcode, color code, volume, SKU or legal permission is inferred.

Neutral placeholders are preferable to copied or reconstructed brand assets. Official imagery can enter only through the asset registry with source, date, license status and production approval.
